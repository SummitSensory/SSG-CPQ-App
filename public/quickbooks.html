QuickBooks Connection

The Summit Sensory Gym CPQ application connects to Summit Sensory Gym’s own QuickBooks Online company so that accepted proposals can be raised as estimates and invoices without being re-keyed.

This is internal business software. It connects to one QuickBooks company — our own — and is not used to access any customer’s QuickBooks data. Accounts are issued by an administrator; there is no public sign-up.

Connecting or reconnecting

Connecting requires an administrator account in the Application.

  1. Sign in at crm.summitsensory.com.
  2. Go to Administration → Integrations.
  3. Choose Connect QuickBooks. You will be sent to Intuit to sign in and authorize the connection.
  4. Approve access. Intuit returns you to the Application and the connection is confirmed on screen.

Reconnecting follows the same steps. It is needed if the authorization was revoked, if it expired through disuse, or when moving between QuickBooks companies.

Disconnecting

You can end the connection from either side. Both take effect immediately.

From the Application: sign in as an administrator, go to Administration → Integrations, and choose Disconnect. The stored authorization is revoked and deleted.

From QuickBooks: in QuickBooks Online, open Settings → Apps (or Manage your apps), find Summit Sensory Gym CPQ, and disconnect it there.

After disconnecting, the Application can no longer read from or write to QuickBooks. Estimates and invoices already created in QuickBooks remain in your books and are unaffected; proposals and orders in the Application remain intact and simply stop syncing.

What the connection accesses

The Application sends customer names and addresses, product and service names and SKUs, and the line items and totals of accepted proposals. It reads back document numbers, payment status and outstanding balances for reconciliation only. Nothing read from QuickBooks overwrites a proposal or a price.
