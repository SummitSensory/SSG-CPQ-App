Privacy Policy

Effective 10 August 2026 · Last updated 10 August 2026

This policy describes how Summit Sensory Gym (“Summit Sensory Gym,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, stores and protects information in the Summit Sensory Gym CPQ application (the “Application”), available at crm.summitsensory.com.

The Application is internal business software. It is used by Summit Sensory Gym employees and authorized contractors to prepare quotes, proposals and orders for sensory gym equipment and related services. It is not offered to the general public, it has no consumer-facing accounts, and it is not available for sign-up.

2. Information the Application holds

Business contact and customer records

The Application stores the business records needed to quote and fulfil a project:

  • Organization details: company name, industry, billing and shipping addresses, and site or room information relevant to an installation.
  • Business contact details for people at those organizations: name, job title, work email address, work phone number, and notes about their role in a project.
  • Opportunity, proposal, order and project records, including line items, quantities, pricing, freight, discounts, payment schedules and internal notes.
  • Product catalog data: part numbers, descriptions, costs, prices, weights, vendors and lead times.

User account information

For each person authorized to use the Application we store a name, work email address, a role that determines what they may see and do, and a hashed password where password sign-in is used. We never store passwords in readable form.

Activity records

The Application keeps an audit trail of significant actions — who created or changed a proposal, who released it, who connected an integration, who changed a user’s role — together with a timestamp. Server logs record request activity for operational and security purposes.

What the Application does not collect

  • No consumer or household data. Contacts are recorded in their professional capacity.
  • No payment card numbers, bank account numbers or payment credentials.
  • No government identifiers such as Social Security numbers.
  • No health information, and no information about the individuals who ultimately use the equipment we supply.
  • No advertising or tracking cookies, no analytics pixels, and no third-party ad networks. The Application sets only the cookies and tokens required to keep a signed-in session working.

3. How the information is used

Information in the Application is used solely to run Summit Sensory Gym’s business: preparing and sending quotes and proposals, managing accepted orders and procurement, issuing invoices through our accounting system, coordinating installations, and maintaining the records required for accounting, warranty and audit purposes.

We do not sell information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We do not use it to train machine-learning models.

4. QuickBooks Online

The Application connects to Summit Sensory Gym’s own QuickBooks Online company through Intuit’s official OAuth 2.0 authorization flow. It does not connect to any other organization’s QuickBooks company, and it does not access QuickBooks data on behalf of our customers.

What we send to QuickBooks

DataPurpose
Customer name, billing and shipping address, primary contact emailCreate or match the customer record an invoice is issued against
Product and service name, SKU, descriptionCreate the corresponding QuickBooks item
Estimate and invoice line items, quantities, amounts, totals and currencyRaise the estimate or invoice for an accepted proposal

What we read back from QuickBooks

DataPurpose
QuickBooks object id and document numberLink a Summit record to the QuickBooks document it produced
Invoice payment status and outstanding balanceDisplay-only reconciliation, so staff can see what has been paid
QuickBooks customer idPrevent duplicate customer records on later syncs

The exchange is deliberately narrow. Summit Sensory Gym’s CPQ is the system of record for what is owed, and QuickBooks is the system of record for the accounting lifecycle after a document exists. Values read from QuickBooks are never written back over proposal or pricing figures.

OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Intuit are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM before being stored. Credentials are supplied to the Application through environment configuration and never appear in source code. A connection can be revoked at any time from within the Application or from the QuickBooks company settings, which immediately ends our access.

5. Other service providers

The Application relies on a small number of providers, each processing data only to deliver its service to us:

  • Vercel — application hosting.
  • Neon — managed PostgreSQL database hosting.
  • Intuit QuickBooks Online — accounting, as described above.
  • monday.com — customer and deal records synchronized with our sales board.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery for account invitations, password resets, proposals and vendor documents.
  • Microsoft Entra ID — optional single sign-on for staff accounts.

6. Security

  • All traffic is served over HTTPS.
  • Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database with encryption at rest.
  • Third-party OAuth tokens are separately encrypted with AES-256-GCM before storage.
  • Access is role-based; each user sees only what their role permits, and financial actions require an explicitly granted permission.
  • Single sign-on is restricted to approved email domains, so an account cannot be self-provisioned from outside the organization.
  • Sessions use short-lived access tokens with separately revocable refresh tokens, and an administrator can revoke a user’s sessions immediately.
  • Writes to the live accounting company are gated behind an explicit configuration flag that is disabled by default.
  • Significant actions are recorded in an audit trail.

No system is perfectly secure, but we review access regularly and keep the number of people and services able to reach production data as small as practical.

7. Retention

Business records — proposals, orders, invoices and the customer records attached to them — are retained for as long as needed to service the customer relationship and to meet accounting, tax, warranty and legal obligations. User accounts are deactivated when someone leaves, while the audit trail of what they did is retained so historical records stay complete and attributable.

8. Your choices and requests

If you are a customer of Summit Sensory Gym and want to know what information we hold about you or your organization, want it corrected, or want a copy, contact us at info@summitsensory.com and we will respond within a reasonable period.

We will honor deletion requests except where a record must be retained for accounting, tax, contractual or legal reasons — an issued invoice, for example, cannot be deleted from our accounting records.

9. Children

The Application is not directed to children and is not used by them. Summit Sensory Gym supplies equipment to clinics, schools and therapy practices, but the Application holds no information about the children who use that equipment.

10. International users

The Application and its data are hosted in the United States. Where we work with customers outside the United States, information is processed in the United States.
